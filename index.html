I am a tenure-track assistant professor in the Department of Statistics at Stanford University. Prior to joining Stanford, I was a Norbert Wiener postdoctoral associate in the Statistics and Data Science Center at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), where I was mentored by Yury Polyanskiy and Philippe Rigollet. I completed my PhD in the Department of Statistics and Data Science at Carnegie Mellon University (CMU), where I was advised by Sivaraman Balakrishnan and Larry Wasserman. Before joining CMU, I received a Bachelor of Science in Mathematics from McGill University, where I was mentored by Abbas Khalili.

I am broadly interested in nonparametric inference and statistical machine learning. My methodological and theoretical research centers around statistical optimal transport, latent variable models, and nonparametric hypothesis testing. Much of my recent work is motivated by interdisciplinary collaborations in the physical sciences, particularly in quantum information science and high energy physics.

My papers can be found below or on my Google Scholar page, and relevant code can be found on GitHub.
